Event Description

Experience the monumental sounds of Coke Studio Bangla live on an unprecedented 360-degree acoustic stage. Featuring celebrated artists from Sylhet, Chittagong, Kushtia, and the international diaspora. High-fidelity surround sound, digital stagecraft, and food courts celebrating Bangladeshi delicacies.

This edition celebrates the rivers of Bengal—Padma, Meghna, Jamuna—through a curated musical journey that blends Baul philosophy, Rabindra sangeet, modern hip-hop, and classical sitar compositions. Art installations depicting river life will flank the walkways.
